Booking se pehle

Optional shortcut: Vadodara utro

Train Vadodara rukti ho toh wahan utarne se Statue of Unity ka drive chhota hoga. Stop aur timing IRCTC par check karo; isse Ahmedabad-based Day 2–3 plan badlega.

Best mahine

November se February mausam thanda-sukha; Rann Utsav dates har saal check karo (2026–27 dates confirm).

Start ka din chuno

Schedule ke mutabik Kanpur ~01:05 aur return ~23:10. Sunday start se Statue of Unity Monday ko nahi padega. Current timings IRCTC par check karo.

Cab lo, khud drive nahi

Day 3–9 ke liye driver wali cab lo. Day 4, 5, 9 long drives hain; jaldi niklo aur raat ki highway driving se bacho.

Mandir ke niyam

Saleeqe ke kapde pehno. Dwarkadhish/Somnath mein phone-bag restrictions ho sakti hain; cloakroom aur timings pehle check karo.

Pehle book karo

Train dates pakki hote hi Statue tickets, White Rann permit/tent aur Gir safari book karo.

Doori aur time road ke andaaze hain. Train ke time aur stops third-party schedules se hain, toh IRCTC pe confirm karo. Mandir aur monument ke timing badalte rehte hain, jaane se pehle check kar lo.
